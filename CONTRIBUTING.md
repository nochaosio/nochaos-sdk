# Contributing

Thank you for helping. The short version:

1. Open an issue before a large change, so we agree on the direction.
2. Keep changes small and tested: `go test ./...` and `gofmt` must pass.
3. Sign your commits off (`git commit -s`). The sign-off is the
   [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/): you
   state that you wrote the change or have the right to submit it.

The contract in this repository is used by every plugin, so a change that
breaks plugins needs a changelog entry saying what authors must do.
